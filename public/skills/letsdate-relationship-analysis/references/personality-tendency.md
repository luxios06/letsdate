# Personality Tendency

The user needs a usable directional judgment, not a report that hides every conclusion behind "insufficient evidence".

## Output rule

- Always produce a best-fit four-letter MBTI tendency when any profile or chat text exists.
- Low evidence changes confidence, alternatives and wording; it does not erase the tendency.
- Use `待观察` only when there is literally no usable text.
- Keep the result non-clinical. It is a communication hypothesis, not a fixed identity or diagnosis.
- State why the best fit was chosen and name the closest alternative types.

## Evidence weighting

Score each axis separately:

- E/I: social initiation, shared activity, external expression versus solitude, reflection and low-stimulation preference.
- S/N: concrete facts, plans and details versus patterns, future possibilities and abstract meaning.
- T/F: logic, rules and trade-offs versus emotional impact, care and relationship values.
- J/P: closure, scheduling and commitment versus flexibility, options and spontaneity.

Use the target person's own messages and profile. Do not use the user's messages to type the other person.

Stronger evidence:

- repeated behavior across more than one topic;
- concrete action rather than one adjective;
- a pattern appearing in both profile and chat.

Weaker evidence:

- one self-description;
- a single short reply;
- culturally common indirectness;
- words quoted from somebody else.

## Confidence

- `较高`: several aligned signals across multiple dimensions and contexts.
- `中`: a clear directional pattern with some mixed evidence.
- `低`: enough for a first-pass tendency, but limited material. Still give the candidate.

## Alternatives

Name one to three alternative types by flipping the axes with the smallest margin. Do not list alternatives that do not logically follow from the dimension scores.

## Communication translation

Turn the tendency into one or two practical implications:

- I: allow processing space and avoid rapid follow-up.
- E: use interactive conversation and shared activity.
- S: provide concrete examples and specific next steps.
- N: explain the bigger direction and meaning first.
- T: make logic, constraints and trade-offs explicit.
- F: acknowledge feelings and relationship impact before solving.
- J: clarify time, ownership and closure.
- P: leave options and flexibility where possible.

## Limits

Do not infer mental-health conditions, protected traits, deception, dangerousness or intent from MBTI-style text signals.
